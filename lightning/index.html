Fractal Lightning

This canvas is grown from pure math: a seed number becomes fractional Brownian discharge paths, the paths branch into smaller copies of themselves, and the glow is light added on top of light. Tap the sky for a new seed — every seed is a different picture, and no two are ever alike. One of them got printed big for a little girl's room wall.

seed 7 · tap the sky

The math: recursive midpoint displacement (fractional Brownian motion), self-similar branching (the collage theorem), and additive glow kernels. Desktop sibling lives in Processing with a 4× print renderer. Inspired by a hand-painted canvas by @peachykayy.
