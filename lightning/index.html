Fractal Lightning

This canvas is grown from pure math: a seed number becomes fractional Brownian discharge paths, the paths branch into smaller copies of themselves, and the glow is light added on top of light. Tap the sky for a new seed — every seed is a different picture, and no two are ever alike. My daughter gets to pick what she wants to make with one of these — she hasn't decided yet.
